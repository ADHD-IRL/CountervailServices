What we run

Three ways to stress test a plan.

Council review

An expert panel for every scenario

A council picked for your scenario reviews the plan, and a required contrarian argues against the consensus.

Pre-mortem

Assume it failed. Find out why.

Dozens of failure stories, grouped into the themes that keep recurring and the one assumption that would sink the plan.

Red team review

A moot court for your plan

A red team attacks, a defender answers, and a judge rules. You see the arguments that survived.

Operating principle

AI proposes. Humans dispose.

Every finding is accepted or rejected by an accountable person. Rules of engagement and data handling are agreed before any work starts, and sensitive work runs in your own or an accredited environment.
